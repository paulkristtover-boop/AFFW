About the Founder

Meet Austine

Austine is a passionate footwear designer and entrepreneur with over a decade of experience in fashion and craftsmanship. Founded in 2018, Austine Fashion Footwears (AFFW) has grown into a trusted name for premium, hand-finished shoes that blend contemporary aesthetics with everyday comfort.

From classic leather oxfords to modern sneakers and statement boots, every pair is selected or designed with attention to materials, silhouette, and lasting quality. Austine’s vision is simple: footwear that elevates your style without compromising on comfort.
